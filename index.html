      The method
      
    Three passes, then get out of the way.
    
      
    Most bad output starts as a vague request. Fix that first — it's cheaper than fixing the code.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    01
    
        
    Say the outcome
    
        
    Not the vibe, not the adjectives. What should be true when this is finished?
    
      
    
      
    
        
    02
    
        
    Add the walls
    
        
    Constraints, edge states, and what must not change. This is where the score moves.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    03
    
        
    Ship it and file it
    
        
    Copy into your assistant. The prompt stays in the library for the next time.
